# Focus Sessions

## Error:

Users may find it difficult to maintain a clear sense of progress while working on a task, especially when a work session is interrupted or completed at a different time than expected.

## Relevant Context

FOCUS.OS includes a Focus Screen where users can work on a selected task. The focus experience is connected to task status and records activity associated with a session.

## Key Observation

A focus timer should be more than a countdown. It should fit into the task workflow so that the user's progress can be reflected in the rest of the application.

## Solution

The focus-session experience was developed to let users start working on a selected task, monitor the session and record its completion or interruption. This connects focused work with the wider task and progress experience.
