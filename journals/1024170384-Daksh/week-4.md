# Progress and Streak Tracking

## Error:

Recording individual completed tasks does not always give users a clear overview of their activity over several days. A separate progress view is useful for showing consistency and daily effort.

## Relevant Context

FOCUS.OS maintains activity information associated with the user's productivity. The application includes daily progress and streak-related displays, along with routine features that support recurring activities.

## Key Observation

Progress indicators should reflect actual user activity. They should provide encouragement while avoiding misleading values or unnecessary pressure to maintain a perfect streak.

## Solution

Activity tracking and daily streak information were incorporated into the progress experience. Routine-related features were also included to support recurring activities. These features provide users with a broader view of their productivity beyond individual task completion.
