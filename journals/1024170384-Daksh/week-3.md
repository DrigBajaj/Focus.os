# PixelPet and User Progress

## Error:

A productivity application that only displays task lists and completion states may provide limited feedback about the user's progress. Users may benefit from having their effort represented in a more engaging and visible way.

## Relevant Context

FOCUS.OS includes a PixelPet companion and progress-related information. The companion experience is connected to user activity and progression data.

## Key Observation

Progress feedback should complement the main productivity workflow. It should encourage users without making them feel penalised when they miss a task or take a break.

## Solution

The PixelPet experience was incorporated as a companion to the task and focus features. Progress-related information, including experience points and companion development, provides an additional way for users to see their activity over time.
